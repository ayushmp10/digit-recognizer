Step 1 of 10

    Hidden means unobserved

    Layers 1 and 2 are called hidden for one reason: their outputs are never part of the training data. Here they are on screen anyway.

    Depth is literal

    Nothing makes a network “deep” except the number of stacked hidden layers. This one has two, so it is barely deep at all, and it still gets 97.8% right.

    Raw scores are not probabilities

    The last layer emits 10 unbounded z values. Softmax exponentiates them and divides by the total, which is the only reason the column of p sums to 1.

    Trained by hand

    ReLU hidden layers, cross-entropy loss, mini-batch SGD with a decaying learning rate and L2. Backprop is written out term by term in network.py.
